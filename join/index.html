Graduate students

Send an email describing your background and what you would like to work on, with a CV and a transcript.

Postdoctoral fellows

Postdoctoral applicants are encouraged to contact the lab. Projects build on the lab’s established research directions, with fellows contributing their own expertise and questions to shape how those directions develop. Send a cover letter describing your research interests and the direction you would want to build on, a CV, and the names of three references.

Undergraduate students

Honours projects and summer placements are offered as space allows. To apply, please send a brief statement of interest, a CV and an unofficial transcript; prior laboratory experience is not required.

Research staff

There are currently no research staff openings. Future positions will be advertised through the University of Ottawa and the CHEO Research Institute. Prospective applicants may send a CV in the meantime to be contacted when a suitable position arises.
