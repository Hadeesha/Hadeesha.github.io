Spatial Biology

Based at the University of Ottawa and the Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario Research Institute (CHEO RI), we connect multi-omic tissue imaging, experimental biology and computational methods to investigate how different molecular components interact to shape cell and tissue function, in development and in disease.
