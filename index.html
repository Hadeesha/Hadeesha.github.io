Understanding tissues in space

We investigate how molecular components interact to shape cell and tissue function, in development and in disease. Lung development and pediatric brain tumour biology are the two applications.

Based at the CHEO Research Institute and the University of Ottawa, we connect tissue imaging, experimental biology and computational analysis to ask how local molecular environments relate to cellular behaviour.
