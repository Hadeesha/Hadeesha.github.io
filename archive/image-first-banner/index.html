Our Research

The lab uses multi-omic imaging and computational methods to study how tissues are organised. Lung development and pediatric tumour biology are the two settings.

The questions follow tissue architecture, including immune and stromal neighbourhoods, in the developing lung and in pediatric glioma and medulloblastoma.
