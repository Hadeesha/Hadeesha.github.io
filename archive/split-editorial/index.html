Hadeesha Piyadasa, PhD

How the developing lung and pediatric brain tumours are organised.

  • Lung development
  • Pediatric tumour biology
  • Spatial multi-omics

Research here uses multi-omic imaging and computational methods to study tissue architecture. The two settings are lung development and pediatric tumour biology, including pediatric glioma and medulloblastoma. Immune and stromal neighbourhoods are part of both.

Research focuses

  1. 01

    Lung development

    Epithelial, immune, and stromal cells in a tissue that is still being built.

  2. 02

    Pediatric tumour biology

    Tissue architecture, the blood-brain barrier, metabolism, and N-glycosylation in pediatric glioma and medulloblastoma.

  3. 03

    Spatial multi-omics

    Proteomics, glycomics, and transcriptomics, kept in place on the tissue.

A further direction, still in development, concerns cognitive and cardiac effects in survivors, together with the Brain-Heart Interconnectome. Read the developing direction. Findings from this direction are not reported here.
